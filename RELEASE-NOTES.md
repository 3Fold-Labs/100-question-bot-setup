# 100-Question AI Agent Setup · v0.0.06

Set the boundaries for your AI agents in one place. Choose a track, answer 100 questions with ALLOW, ASK, DENY, or Doesn't apply, and download a Markdown policy to give to the agents you use with a three-line quick start that asks the agent to follow it and confirm it read it.

- **Builder** (I build with AI): for anyone who codes or builds with AI using Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, and similar agents. Covers paid services, code and Git, live data and users, deploying, secrets and logins, your computer, and publishing.
- **Personal** (I use AI in my everyday life): for Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and other everyday assistants and agents. Covers shopping and selling, messages and calls, calendar, personal information, accounts and security, files and photos, home devices, family, health and legal matters, and social media.

Each track downloads as a full policy (`my-agent-policy-<track>.md`), a compact policy with the same operating rules in fewer words (`my-agent-policy-<track>-compact.md`), and an answer backup (`my-agent-answers-<track>.json`). Suggested answers stay marked until you review them. Sections that do not apply turn off as a whole and collapse to their heading. **Next unanswered** finds what is left, **Test your agent** gives three checks built from your own answers, and **Finished both tracks?** lists the questions you answered differently in each track. The blank worksheet (`ai-agent-rules.html`) holds both tracks and no answers.

On the Builder track, your answers also become settings files that Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, and Cursor apply themselves, with each file's save location and a way to check it loaded. Rules in a tool's settings cost no tokens. A lean policy (`my-agent-policy-builder-lean.md`) carries only the rules the tools cannot enforce, and every policy download shows an estimate of the tokens it adds to each message. Tool settings are a strong guardrail, not a lock.

**[Open the worksheet](https://3fold-labs.github.io/ai-agent-rules/)** or download the attached blank HTML and open it in your browser. No account, server, API key, or MCP connection is required. Answers save in your browser only, and the sticky bar shows whether they are saved. Downloads land in your browser's downloads folder, usually Downloads, or the Files app on iPhone. Keep the answer backup to restore or move your answers.
