# 100-Question AI Agent Setup · v0.0.7

Set the boundaries for your AI agents in one place. Choose a track, answer 100 questions with ALLOW, ASK, DENY, or Doesn't apply, and download a Markdown policy to give to the agents you use with a three-line quick start that asks the agent to follow it and confirm it read it by listing up to three things it will never do.

- **Builder** (I build with AI): for anyone who codes or builds with AI using Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, and similar agents. Covers paid services, code and Git, live data and users, deploying, secrets and logins, your computer, and publishing.
- **Personal** (I use AI in my everyday life): for Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and other everyday assistants and agents. Covers shopping and selling, messages and calls, calendar, personal information, accounts and security, files and photos, home devices, family, health and legal matters, and social media.

**Download my agent policy** gives you the full policy, and **Save my answers to a file** sits beside it so you can bring your answers back with **Load my answers from a file**, on this device or another. **More options** holds the compact policy, the quick start text, and **Copy policy**. On the Builder track, **Coding tool settings** can also generate settings files for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, and Cursor. See [Coding tool settings](./README.md#coding-tool-settings) in the README.

**[Open the worksheet](https://3fold-labs.github.io/ai-agent-rules/)** or download the attached blank HTML and open it in your browser. No account, server, API key, or MCP connection is required. Answers save in your browser only.
