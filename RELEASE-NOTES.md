# 100-Question AI Agent Setup · v0.0.05

Set the boundaries for your AI agents in one place. Choose a track, answer 100 questions with ALLOW, ASK, DENY, or Doesn't apply, and download a Markdown policy to give to the agents you use with the instruction: **"Follow this policy."**

- **Builder** (I build with AI): for anyone who codes or builds with AI using Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, and similar agents. Covers paid services, code and Git, live data and users, deploying, secrets and logins, your computer, and publishing.
- **Personal** (I use AI in my everyday life): for Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and other everyday assistants and agents. Covers shopping and selling, messages and calls, calendar, personal information, accounts and security, files and photos, home devices, family, health and legal matters, and social media.

Each track downloads as a full policy (`my-agent-policy-<track>.md`), a short policy (`my-agent-policy-<track>-short.md`), and an answer backup (`my-agent-answers-<track>.json`). Sections that do not apply can be turned off as a whole. The blank worksheet (`100-question-bot-setup.html`) holds both tracks and no answers.

**[Open the worksheet](https://3fold-labs.github.io/100-question-bot-setup/)** or download the attached blank HTML and open it in your browser. No account, server, API key, or MCP connection is required. Answers save in your browser only. Downloads land in your browser's downloads folder, usually Downloads, or the Files app on iPhone. Keep the answer backup to restore or move your answers.
